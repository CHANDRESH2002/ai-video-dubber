Where the edge actually is

Current experiment

Does a per-speaker voice reference carry emotion, or only identity?

XTTS-v2 clones a speaker's voice from one reference clip — traced into the model source earlier: the clip gets pooled into a single fixed-size embedding for identity, and only its first six seconds shape delivery style, regardless of clip length. Every line from a speaker currently reuses the same blended reference, so whatever emotion the original line carried isn't reaching synthesis.

Today's test: pulled three lines from a genuinely emotional clip (The Bear, a monologue about childhood and anxiety) that a human would call clearly different in register, and ran them through emotion2vec+ two ways.

Label match
3 / 3 → neutral
top prediction never changed, only confidence did (56–99.99%)
Cosine similarity
0.91 – 0.93
near-different and near-same pairs landed in the same tight band

Neither comparison separated lines a person would call obviously different — likely because the source is quiet, naturalistic acting, and most emotion models are tuned on overt expression (raised voices, crying, laughing). Next: test against unambiguous emotion to isolate whether the limitation is the model or this clip, before committing to a metric design or touching the synthesis fix.
